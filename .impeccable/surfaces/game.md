# Game surface

Primary target: web/app.js. Related targets: style.css, web/renderer.js, web/viewport.js, web/navigation.js.

Mode: Experience. Refine the existing illustrated world into a full-viewport tactical dungeon. The expedition is a fixed 100dvh canvas with a persistent HUD, no site header/footer and no page scroll. Health/energy sit top-left, floor/location top-center, minimap and menu top-right, movement/actions at the bottom. The player remains centered in the unobstructed play area. Entry and resume keep the ordinary browser window. Native fullscreen is requested only from its explicit HUD or pause control; all play controls work in ordinary browser viewport mode.

Signature: a lantern-lit map with large illustrated sprites, announced danger tiles and a contextual gold action for an adjacent enemy or object. Click-to-walk only uses explored safe cells and stops when enemies appear. Inventory, lore, help and pause are accessible overlays that stop travel. Portrait and short landscape retain all actions; mobile minimap is expandable. Preserve saved games and the refuge identity. Only one web edition is offered.

Extension direction: make meaningful choices readable within the established lantern-lit expedition journal. Keep charcoal, ivory, gold and jade, Georgia world headings, system UI annotations, quiet borders and painterly ruins. The story remains preparation, reading danger, choosing a relic, preserving progress and retrying. The refuge opens with the existing illustrated entry; in play, character, resources, map and actions share the first viewport. This remains the existing static native-JavaScript and Canvas surface.

Expedition preparation adds three oath sidegrades below the hero and a collapsed optional seed field. Selected oaths use the existing gold border and wash with explicit attribute tradeoffs; the neutral oath remains the first-expedition recommendation. Oaths stack on mobile. The seed accepts integers from 0 to 4294967295, reports invalid input beside the field and leaves a blank value random. Pause and the journal expose a copy action; the end screen can replay the same seed, class and oath using the current permanent progress. Seed copy remains optional when the clipboard is unavailable.

Combat keeps six persistent actions. Esperar offers +2 energy without protection; Guardia offers +4 armor and +1 energy for the turn, with consequence text allowed to wrap at narrow widths. The danger warning shows maximum announced damage at the player's current cell, the guarded amount and a lethal-risk label. This warning covers already announced attacks only; unannounced melee and later enemy decisions remain outside it. Keep that scope clear in the help and contextual explanation.

Relic choices identify Ofensiva, Supervivencia and Canalización with distinct existing icons. Each preserves its effect description and selection action, adds applicable before/after stat rows, and shows the number of carried relics in its path. On mobile, choices become stacked rows and the dialog scrolls internally. The next destination pairs a region painting with floor and recovery information. Six paintings from resources/art/yala-domains.png also illustrate the refuge's descent track.

Feedback remains inside the world: finite hit impulses, damage/healing text, skill rings, dash traces and defeat embers. Presentation does not consume the engine's randomness. Reduced motion responds to preference changes, keeps feedback still and clears it with timers instead of an animation-frame loop. Hidden tabs cancel active effects. Synthesized cues in web/audio.js are opt-in from the refuge sound control, off by default, and stop when sound is disabled or the tab is hidden.

World comparison: DESIGN.md and .impeccable/design.json remain unchanged. The extension reuses their palette, type families, corner language, focus treatment and flat tonal hierarchy. Known preexisting drift: DESIGN.md's Layout paragraph and the sidecar viewport description still say entry requests native fullscreen; the current surface and DESIGN.md's later clarification correctly reserve it for the explicit HUD or pause control. Extension-specific documentation lag also remains in their fixed 850ms feedback and creation-only reduced-motion descriptions, rarity-led reward description and older Guard example without its energy consequence. These differences do not authorize replacing the visual world or its token sources.

Required verification: viewport-sized captures at 1440×900, 390×844, 844×390, 320×568 and actual user width 924, plus native fullscreen; no page overflow or clipped essential controls. Confirm resize/picking, pause/resume, keyboard/touch, safe travel cancellation and save reload. For the extension, also check oath tradeoffs, blank/invalid/valid seed entry, copy fallback and replay, announced-damage scope, reward descriptions/stat rows, reduced-motion changes, hidden-tab cleanup and sound opt-in. Deliver to `master`, the explicitly requested primary branch.
